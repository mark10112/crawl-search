Main comparison: Mac production auto/balanced routing versus connected Firecrawl scrape, each off/prime/hit, one observation per document/phase. Off bypasses URL and page result caches; prime enables cache on the first request; hit repeats the same options. Record actual cache metadata rather than infer hit from speed.
